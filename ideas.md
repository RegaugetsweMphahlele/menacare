# MenaCare Design Exploration

## Three Directions Considered

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
|---|---|---:|
| Soft Orbit | A buoyant, toy-like care universe that makes sensitive health subjects feel safe and approachable. | 0.07 |
| Storybook Sanctuary | A hand-illustrated calm space shaped by rounded forms, flowers, and gentle guidance. | 0.03 |
| Confident Compass | A structured, editorial dashboard using clear hierarchy and practical resource cues. | 0.09 |

## Chosen Direction: Soft Orbit

### Design Movement

**Soft Orbit** combines tactile 3D product illustration with the optimistic clarity of contemporary youth wellbeing design. It makes MenaCare feel like a comforting companion rather than a clinical tracker.

### Core Principles

1. Make every health action feel emotionally safe, clearly labelled, and low-pressure.
2. Use floating, rounded layers and orbiting micro-illustrations to suggest care that surrounds the user.
3. Contrast an expressive, story-led public landing page with a focused, task-led app dashboard.
4. Keep the experience dignified and direct: warm language, useful information, and no fear-based visual cues.

### Color Philosophy

White creates breathing room and trust. **Carnation Pink** is the encouraging, action-forward signal; **Dark Neon Purple** carries supportive warmth and brand distinction; **Deep Blue** grounds critical navigation and information; and **Coral** softens secondary spaces. The colors appear in decisive blocks and tactile highlights rather than generic gradients.

### Layout Paradigm

The landing page follows an editorial journey of large asymmetrical scenes, offset cards, and stacked ribbons. The app uses a left navigation rail on large screens and a fixed lower navigation on mobile, with focal content gathered in a spacious central canvas and contextual wellbeing cards orbiting it.

### Signature Elements

1. A flowing butterfly motif repeated in calm, subtle details.
2. Tactile 3D objects—pads, backpacks, maps, books, and speech bubbles—set against generous white space.
3. Soft orbit rings and dotted pathways to link health steps, education, and support.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should reassure first and ask second. Action cards respond with a small lift, forms progress in one clear step at a time, and support routes remain visible from every relevant view.

### Animation

Floating ornamentation may drift very slowly, while transitions use 160–260 ms ease-out motion. Cards lift only slightly on hover, buttons compress to 97% on press, and onboarding slides enter at 95% scale with an opacity transition. Motion is disabled for reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

**Lato** carries headings with a friendly, confident weight (700–900) and soft line breaks. **Montserrat** handles labels, navigation, descriptions, and instructional body text (400–700). Headings are compact and editorial; body content uses generous line height for calm readability.

### Brand Essence

**MenaCare is a friendly, private guide that gives young people practical menstrual-health confidence, one safe step at a time.**

**Personality:** reassuring, bright, capable.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are encouraging without patronising; CTAs are specific and gentle; microcopy should describe the next action plainly.

> “Your body has a rhythm. Let’s learn yours.”

> “Need pads today? We’ll help you find a nearby option.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The supplied butterfly logo is the primary wordmark. The brand mark appears as a bolder flowing butterfly icon in navigation, utility moments, and the favicon.

## Style Decisions

The star motif has been removed. The visual language now relies on the butterfly, hearts, rounded dots, soft orbit rings, and tactile objects for personality.

### Signature Brand Color

**Carnation Pink — #FF99D8** is the unmistakable MenaCare action color.
